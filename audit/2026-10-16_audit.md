### 3. Reproducibility

**Strength — eval is held out from construction.** The decision instrument
(`dilemmas_v2/v3/v4`) is independent of what builds the interventions:
`neutral_pairs.json` feeds CAA extraction (`steering.extract_vector`) *and* LoRA
training (`lora.prep_jsonl` writes `stoic_text`), while the dilemmas are a
separate frozen set. v4 items are authored fresh. So "LoRA moves the dilemmas" is
not measured on its own training text. State this — it is a real methodological
strength a reviewer will want to see.

**Seeds:**
- Pair sampling is seeded and recorded (`stoic/pairs.py:68,80` — `seed=613`). ✓
- Sampled judge generation is seeded (`torch.manual_seed(seed)`, seeds 0–4). ✓
- Decision eval is deterministic (greedy logits) — no seed needed. ✓
- **Gap (S3-level):** LoRA training sets **no seed** — `lora.train()` has no
  `set_seed`/`manual_seed`, and `models/control_training_env.json` records
  torch/transformers/peft/GPU but no training seed. So the adapters are not
  bit-reproducible *from scratch*. **Mitigated:** adapters are frozen,
  SHA-checksummed (`data/MANIFEST.sha256`), HF-hosted, and the decision eval on
  them is deterministic — the *published result* is reproducible-from-artifact;
  only a retrain (and Pass B) would drift. Worth one caveat line in the write-up.

**torch pin:** `pyproject.toml` pins torch 2.5.1 (uninstallable on Py3.13 here),
runs happen on 2.8.0; per-item fp16 drift up to 4.3e-3; magnitude stats
reproduce to ~4 dp, sign test does not. Fully disclosed in `decisions.md` and
`CLAUDE.md`. Not a flag — it is a model for how to disclose an environment
confound.
